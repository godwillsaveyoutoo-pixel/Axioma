09 · Interaction contract

Large targets, restrained visuals, explicit state

48 px target baseline, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support and state that never relies on colour alone.

Keyboard test: tab through this gallery. Focus is deliberately more visible than hover. Touch test: controls keep a 48 px minimum target even when their visible icon or underline is much smaller.
